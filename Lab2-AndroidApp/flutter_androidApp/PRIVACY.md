# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FAP Attendance ("the App") handles information when used by lecturers and authorized university staff to manage classroom attendance.

   This App is a student coursework project and is not an official product of
   FPT University or the FAP portal.
## 1. Data We Collect

Depending on the enabled features, the App may process:

- Google account information needed to identify an authorized user, such as name, email address, and profile identifier.
- Class and course information, including subject, class, room, schedule, and session details.
- Student attendance information, including student name, student identifier, attendance status, cumulative absence, and lecturer notes.
- Basic technical information required to operate and secure the App, such as error logs and authentication events.

The current desktop prototype uses mock sign-in and mock attendance data. Google account authentication and Google Sheets integration will only process data after those integrations are enabled and authorized.
The desktop app uses Google OAuth user authentication and reads or writes attendance data only after the signed-in user grants Google Sheets access. Unimplemented navigation areas are not represented as active data-processing features.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Authenticate authorized lecturers and staff.
- Display and manage class rosters and attendance sessions.
- Save, update, and retrieve attendance records.
- Calculate attendance rates and cumulative absence warnings.
- Generate attendance summaries and reports when requested.
- Maintain, troubleshoot, and secure the App.

We do not use Google user data for advertising, profiling, or unrelated purposes.

## 3. Google API and Google Sheets Access

When Google integration is enabled, the App may request access to the Google account and Google Sheets resources necessary to provide attendance functionality. The App only uses the requested permissions for the features described above.

The App does not sell Google user data. We do not transfer Google user data to advertising platforms, data brokers, or unrelated third parties. Google user data is not used to develop or train generalized artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

Users can revoke the App's Google access at any time from their Google Account security settings. Revoking access may prevent Google-connected features from working.

The App's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs
will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use
requirements.

## 4. Data Sharing

Attendance data is shared only with:

- The authorized university personnel and users of the relevant class or course.
- Service providers that help operate the App, only when necessary and under appropriate confidentiality and security obligations.
- Public authorities when required by applicable law.

We do not sell personal information.

## 5. Data Storage and Retention

Attendance records may be stored in the university's authorized Google Sheets or other configured storage. Data is retained only for as long as needed for academic administration, university recordkeeping, security, dispute resolution, or legal obligations.

The university or an authorized administrator may request correction or deletion of information, subject to applicable academic recordkeeping requirements.

## 6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users must keep their Google account credentials and any access tokens confidential and must report suspected unauthorized access promptly.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for lecturers and authorized university staff. It is not directed to children, and it is not knowingly designed to collect information directly from children.

## 8. Your Choices and Requests

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information processed by the App by contacting the administrator listed below. You may also revoke Google authorization through your Google Account settings.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, its integrations, or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be published at the same public URL and will include a new effective date.
